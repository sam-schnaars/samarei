PRIVACY

Sammy Word privacy policy · effective November 27, 2026

The short version: Sammy Word has no accounts and no analytics, and Sammy Word itself shows no ads. Most of what it remembers stays on your phone. The one exception is Caption the Meme: captions you post (and the name you add, if any) go to our caption board so other players can see and vote on them, and are deleted after 14 days.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

To show check marks and your streak, Sammy Word saves which games you finished each day and the score you got, for about the last 60 days. This is stored only in the app on your device. It's never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

SCORES YOU SEND

When you finish a game, Sammy Word puts your result (the game, your score, the puzzle number and the round details) into an iMessage bubble in the conversation you're in. It's delivered by Apple's Messages to the people in that conversation, the same as any message you send. We never receive it.

CAPTION THE MEME

When you post a caption, we store the caption, the name you typed (optional; leave it blank to stay anonymous), your vote, and any reports you make, together with a random ID that the app creates on your device (stored only as a scrambled version). We use this to show today's captions, count votes, stop repeat voting, and hide captions that break the rules. We also briefly use your IP address to limit how fast captions and votes can be sent, so the board can't be spammed; it isn't stored with your caption. Everything on the board is deleted automatically after 14 days, and we can remove a caption sooner if you ask. The board runs on Vercel and Upstash (Redis). We don't sell this data or use it for advertising.

PICTURES AND VIDEOS

Caption the Meme's pictures load from Wikimedia Commons and Scroll Recall's videos stream from NASA. Like any website, those servers see that a picture or video was requested from your device; we don't send them anything else.

THE GAMES' WEBSITES

Some daily games are websites made by other people. Magnitudle and 100 of Us play inside the Sammy Word app with their creators' permission, and Krillion and Eraly open in Safari. While you play, those sites work as they do in Safari: they may show ads and use cookies and analytics, under their own privacy policies (linked above). Sammy Word reads only your finished result from the page, on your device, so it can send your score.

CHILDREN

Sammy Word isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

CHANGES AND CONTACT
