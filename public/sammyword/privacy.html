PRIVACY

Sammy Word privacy policy · effective November 23, 2026

The short version: Sammy Word has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads and no analytics. It doesn't collect your personal information. What it remembers stays on your phone.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

To show check marks and your streak, Sammy Word saves which games you finished each day and the score you got, for about the last 60 days. This is stored only in the app on your device. It's never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

SCORES YOU SEND

When you finish a game, Sammy Word puts your result (the game, your score, the puzzle number and the round details) into an iMessage bubble in the conversation you're in. It's delivered by Apple's Messages to the people in that conversation, the same as any message you send. We never receive it.

THE GAMES' WEBSITES

The daily games are websites made by other people, and Sammy Word opens them in an in-app browser: Krillion, Magnitudle, 100 of Us and Eraly. While you play, those sites work as they do in Safari: they may use cookies, advertising and analytics, and may save data in the in-app browser, under their own privacy policies (linked above). Sammy Word reads only your finished result from the page, on your device, so it can send your score.

CHILDREN

Sammy Word isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone.

CHANGES AND CONTACT

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: samschnaars@berkeley.edu.
