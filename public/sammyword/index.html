SAMMY WORD

A game of the day for your group chat, inside iMessage.

Every day Sammy Word features one daily game. Play it without leaving Messages, and your score drops into the chat as a pixel-art picture so your friends can try to beat it.

  • A new featured game every day, plus the rest under “Also today”
  • Your score goes to the chat as soon as you finish
  • Streaks for playing the game of the day

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Open a conversation in Messages.
  2. Tap + next to the text field, then More, and pick Sammy Word.
  3. Tap PLAY on today's game.

Sammy Word is made by Sam Schnaars. The featured games are made by their own creators and open in an in-app browser; Sammy Word isn't affiliated with them.
