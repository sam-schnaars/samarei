FINDING THE APP

Sammy Word lives in Messages. Open a chat, tap + next to the text field, then More, and choose Sammy Word. Swipe up on it to go full screen.

MY SCORE DIDN'T SEND

When Messages doesn't allow an automatic send, the score bubble waits in the text field. Tap the blue send arrow to post it. You can also tap the green score button at the top of the game to send it again.

A GAME WON'T LOAD

The games load from their creators' websites, so they need an internet connection. Tap RELOAD on the loading screen. If a site is down, try again later.

QUESTIONS ABOUT A GAME

The daily games are made by their own creators. For questions about a puzzle or its answer, contact the game directly: Krillion, Magnitudle, 100 of Us, Eraly.
